## 1. Information Collection and Local Data Storage

### A. Network Measurements
The app performs network requests solely to measure your internet connection performance:
- Download Speed & Upload Speed
- Network Latency (Ping), Jitter, and Packet Loss
- Connection Type (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data) and ISP (Internet Service Provider) Name

**Local Storage:** All speed test results, measurement history, and preferences are stored **100% locally on your device**. We do not operate external servers to collect or store your test history or network logs.

### B. Personal Data
We do not require user account registration, name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location. We do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

## 3. Device Permissions

The application requests the following Android permissions to perform its core functions:
- `INTERNET`: Required to execute speed measurements against public speed test servers.
- `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to detect connection type (Wi-Fi or Cellular).
- `AD_ID`: Used by Google AdMob to deliver appropriate advertising.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Optional permission used to deliver background test alerts when network speeds fall below user-defined plan thresholds.
